## Findings

- **Theater — entrance orientation is questionable.** `city-single-Theater-front.png`
  shows a nearly blank long facade; the visible long marquee/door-like strip is
  on the opposite side in `city-single-Theater-rear.png`. In the city,
  `city-placed-Theater-front.png` presents the blank wall toward the camera,
  while `city-placed-Theater-rear.png` shows the marquee side toward the road.
  If front is intended to face the primary approach, the labeled/front-facing
  approach reads as the service side. No path from sidewalk to the entrance is
  visible in the captures.
- **Beach — no pedestrian connection is apparent.** The isolated captures show
  a broad, simple sand-and-water rectangle (`city-single-Beach-top.png`). In
  placed views, the beach pad lies beside the street edge, but no walkway from
  the sidewalk into the beach is visible (`city-placed-Beach-front.png`,
  `city-placed-Beach-top.png`). This is a visual access gap; actual walkability
  is not established by these images.
- **Harbor and Marina — edges read as abrupt slabs.** Their placed top views show
  rectangular water and hard apron edges meeting the surrounding grass without
  a visible shoreline transition (`city-placed-Harbor-top.png`,
  `city-placed-Marina-top.png`). The docks and small boats are present, but
  finer mooring/support contact is too small to confirm from the sheets.
- **Building entries and sidewalk access are generally unproven.** Most placed
  buildings sit on isolated pale rectangular pads surrounded by grass; captured
  views show nearby roads/sidewalks but no connecting path to entrances. This is
  clearest for Bank (`city-placed-Bank-front.png`), CoffeeHouse
  (`city-placed-CoffeeHouse-front.png`), and Hotel
  (`city-placed-Hotel-front.png`). The captures do not prove whether a route
  exists outside the camera framing.

## Overall visual read

The 13 silhouettes are stable across the isolated angles, with no obvious
floating primary structures or major self-intersections visible in the
contact sheets. The fire station garage bays, playground equipment, monument,
boats, and multi-floor hotel remain legible in their placed views. Small facade
details and thin supports are often too small to judge conclusively. No
obvious landmark-to-landmark overlap is apparent in the five placed views;
these views do not establish clearance for every camera or player route.
